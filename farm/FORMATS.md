# Format Farm — Matua Bill

A format has two parts: a **mechanic** (Fxx: what the slides do) and a **skin** (Vx: how the slides look). They are logged separately so the performance loop can tell a weak hook from a weak look.

All current templates: 1080×1350 portrait, 8 slides, slide counter pill top-right, small caps label under each line.

Performance source: Metricool, Instagram, pulled 2026-09-24. Posts less than 3 days old are still growing.

## F01 — Truth List
**Status:** SCALE · **Skin:** V1 · **Visual reference:** `refs/F01-truth-list.jpg`
- **Best for:** grief, shame, anger, self-trust, boundaries. Any topic with a lot of lines people want to quote.
- **Hook formula:** a short line that names the feeling in a new way ("[Feeling] is [unexpected definition].") Raw language is allowed.
- **Slides:** 1 hook truth → 2–7 standalone truths, each quotable on its own and each going one layer deeper → 8 save/send prompt aimed at a specific person ("Send it to someone sitting with ___").
- **Copy:** ultra-short (1–2 lines a slide). One *italic* phrase per slide.
- **Swipe:** each slide is a new line that could be screenshotted on its own, so people swipe to collect them.
- **Payoff:** permission ("This love is still here…") and a named share target.
- **Share/save:** people send the one slide that is "them" to someone else.
- **Production template:** `[REDEFINE FEELING] → [6 × ONE-LINE TRUTH, escalating intimacy] → [SEND TO SOMEONE WHO ___]`
- **Performance (n≈15, 6–16 Sep):** best "Grief is love that has nowhere to go." 25.1k views · 200 shares · 295 saves. "You stopped listening to yourself…" 26.4k · 109 sh. "That anger was the only thing that ever stood up for you." 18.1k · 191 sh. Typical 8–10k · 25–55 shares. **Best carousel format for shares.**

## F02 — Pattern Arc ("You learned to…")
**Status:** ITERATE · **Skin:** V1 · **Visual reference:** `refs/F02-pattern-arc.jpg`
- **Best for:** survival patterns, people-pleasing, hypervigilance, childhood origins.
- **Hook formula:** a present-day situation → the urge it triggers → where it came from → an adult reframe.
- **Slides:** 1 situation → 2 the urge → 3 origin ("…as a kid was not safe, so you learned…") → 4 reframe → 5–7 permissions → 8 "Let love guide us."
- **Copy:** medium (2–3 lines).
- **Swipe:** "why do I do this?", with the answer arriving on slide 3.
- **Payoff:** a permission, then the sign-off. **Weak spot:** the last slide is a sign-off, not a payoff, and there's no share target.
- **Production template:** `[SITUATION] → [URGE] → [ORIGIN] → [REFRAME] → [3 × PERMISSION] → [SIGN-OFF]`
- **Performance (n=4 with "You learned to…" hooks, 18–23 Sep):** 3.2k–6.6k views · 1–22 shares. **About half the views of F01 and much weaker on shares.** A third-person version run in June ("Some people learned love was inconsistent… Not because you're broken. Because your nervous system adapted.") did 21–25k · 85–116 shares. **Iterate:** use the third-person "Some people learned…" hook, end on "not broken, adapted", and make the last slide a share prompt. (Need a screenshot of a June carousel as the visual reference.)
- **Note:** about 135 queued posts (24 Sep–30 Nov) are F02/F03 in the V1 skin.

## F03 — Scene → Reframe → Tool
**Status:** HOLD · **Skin:** V1 · **Visual reference:** `refs/F03-scene-reframe-tool.jpg`
- **Best for:** panic, triggers, anxiety spikes, 3am thoughts. Topics with a practical tool.
- **Hook formula:** a specific time or place plus a bodily moment ("Three in the morning and…").
- **Slides:** 1 scene → 2 body symptoms → 3 "but you're safe" → 4 name the mechanism → 5 reframe line → 6 the tool (breath or grounding steps) → 7 reassurance → 8 sign-off.
- **Copy:** medium.
- **Swipe:** recognition ("that's me"), then relief.
- **Payoff:** a usable tool. The strongest save mechanic in the farm.
- **Production template:** `[SCENE] → [SYMPTOMS] → [SAFE] → [MECHANISM] → [REFRAME] → [TOOL STEPS] → [REASSURE] → [SAVE THIS FOR 3AM]`
- **Performance:** not isolated yet. Tag it when it goes out.

## F04 — Step Teach (AFPR)
**Status:** SCALE (reach) / ITERATE (shares) · **Skin:** V5 · **Visual reference:** `refs/F04-step-teach.jpg`
- **Best for:** Four Steps teaching: one step, one common mistake, or one misconception per carousel.
- **Hook formula:** a correction ("Step one is not fix it. Step one is *name it*.")
- **Slides:** 1 correction hook → 2 what the step is → 3 what it isn't (quoted self-talk) → 4 the true version → 5 reassurance → 6 body-level why → 7 stakes → 8 "*Acknowledge.* Feel. Process. Release."
- **Copy:** short-medium, left-aligned. The long teaching goes in the caption with "Try this" and a question.
- **Swipe:** a myth is corrected, so you keep going to get the real version.
- **Payoff:** the framework mantra, with the current step in italics.
- **Production template:** `[NOT X, IT'S Y] → [DEFINE] → [NOT THIS] → [THIS] → [YOU'RE NOT WEAK] → [BODY WHY] → [STAKES] → [A. F. P. R.]`
- **Performance (n=10, 13–21 Sep):** average ~14.6k views, top 21.6k ("Release is not forgiveness"). The **highest-reach carousel family.** Shares are low (1–26), except "Step one… name it" (117 sh · 156 sv). **Iterate:** end on a share prompt, and put comment keywords (START) in the caption.
- **Note:** all 10 are used up. No AFPR posts are queued.

## F05 — Framework Walk (4 Steps × topic)
**Status:** HOLD (untested) · **Skin:** V3 · **Visual reference:** `refs/F05-framework-walk.jpg`
- **Best for:** running one emotion (grief, anger, shame, fear…) through all four steps.
- **Slides:** 1 hook about the emotion → 2 Acknowledge → 3 Feel → 4–5 Process → 6–7 Release → 8 "Acknowledge. Feel. Process. Release."
- **Copy:** medium, with a "Step: …" prefix.
- **Payoff:** a full map for one emotion, so people save it.
- **Production template:** `[EMOTION HOOK] → [A: …] → [F: …] → [P: …] → [P: …] → [R: …] → [R: …] → [A. F. P. R.]`
- **Stock:** 20 rendered (`campaign-4steps-c01…c20`), never posted on @matua_bill. **c06 Grief is ready for the Grief container.**

## F06 — Behaviour Mirror
**Status:** HOLD (untested) · **Skin:** V2 · **Visual reference:** `refs/F06-behaviour-mirror.jpg`
- **Best for:** men's mental health. Aim at recognisable behaviour, not feelings.
- **Hook formula:** "When [pressure], you [behaviour]."
- **Slides:** 1 behaviour → 2 concrete evidence list (shed, car, phone) → 3 the story he tells himself → 4 what others feel → 5 myth-bust → 6 what loved ones want → 7 a small action → 8 a three-word imperative.
- **Copy:** ultra-short, heavy type.
- **Share:** partners and mates send it to "him".
- **Production template:** `[WHEN X, YOU Y] → [EVIDENCE] → [HIS STORY] → [THEIR VIEW] → [MYTH-BUST] → [WHAT THEY WANT] → [SMALL STEP] → [IMPERATIVE]`
- **Stock:** 20 rendered (`campaign-mens-c01…c20`). **Supporting signal:** the single-image post "Toxic masculinity taught too many that tears were weakness" did 42.4k views · 158 shares · 66 follows, the best follow driver on the page.

## F07 — Permission Spell
**Status:** HOLD (untested) · **Skin:** V4 · **Visual reference:** `refs/F07-permission-spell.jpg`
- **Best for:** rest, worth, boundaries, gentle affirmations. The quiet slot of the day.
- **Slides:** 1 "You are allowed to ___" → 2 origin ("If ___ was made conditional…") → 3–6 variations → 7 "Say it until it lands: I am ___" → 8 "You are allowed."
- **Copy:** short, airy.
- **Payoff:** a mantra, so people save it.
- **Production template:** `[ALLOWED TO X] → [ORIGIN] → [4 × VARIATION] → [SAY IT] → [YOU ARE ALLOWED.]`
- **Stock:** 20 rendered (`campaign-spells-c01…c20`).
