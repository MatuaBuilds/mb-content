# CTA Canon

Comment triggers only. Never "link in bio". One primary CTA per carousel. Don't stack all three unless there is a specific reason.

| Keyword | Use when | Sends | Caption line |
|---|---|---|---|
| START | The person needs a clear visual framework, or wants to know where to begin | Full Four Steps to Healing PDF | Comment START and I'll send you the free Four Steps to Healing guide. |
| GRIEF | The post is specifically about grief, loss, missing someone, waves of grief, or moving through grief | Grief support resource + Release by Water audio | Comment GRIEF and I'll send you the free grief support I made. |
| BREATHE | The person needs immediate regulation or grounding, or something they can do right now | Free guided breath | Comment BREATHE and I'll send you the free guided breath. |

Selection rule: visual framework / where do I start → START · grief-specific support → GRIEF · immediate regulation → BREATHE.

Caption order: hook line → (optional comment question) → "Save this carousel for a day you need it." → CTA line → "Let love guide us. ❤️" → hashtags.

Needs: the DM automation must trigger on all posts for each keyword.
